My Creative Journey

Scroll down to travel through the major stations and milestones of my photography career.

🚂
01
2011 — Station Departure

The First Click

It all started with an old mechanical film camera gifted by my grandfather. Walking through empty alleyways, I learned the magic of waiting for the right light and framing a raw emotion before it vanished forever.

02
2015 — Junction City

The Urban Chaos

Moving to New York City was a sensory explosion. The subway lights, rushing crowds, and midnight neon signs shaped my style into moody, cinematic street photography where every stranger had a profound story.

03
2018 — Express Terminal

Studio & Shadows

Stepping into controlled studio environments taught me precision. Sculpting portraits with shadows, softboxes, and high-end editorial concepts transformed how I capture human character and corporate branding.

04
2022 — Global Express

The Worldwide Lens

From drone photography over emerald coastlines to covering global documentary assignments, my camera became my passport. Exhibitions in 15 galleries cemented my dedication to visual storytelling.

05
Present & Beyond

The Infinite Shutter

Today, as an independent freelance photographer, the journey never stops. Every new project is a fresh track heading into uncharted artistic territories. Let’s build the next chapter together.
